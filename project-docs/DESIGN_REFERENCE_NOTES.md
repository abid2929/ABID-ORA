## What makes them feel premium
1. One hero idea per page: a single large subject (3D object, cut-out person, product) with huge type behind or beside it.
   - Marcus Vane (founder site): a cut-out portrait standing in front of a giant red name. Directly relevant to Abid's Founder page.
   - Creative Director: portrait floating on a blurred coloured light field.
   - Lumora: grey portrait, oversized faded wordmark at the bottom.
2. Very restrained navigation: small wordmark left, 3–5 links centred or right, one pill CTA.
3. Typography: one display face used very large (often 120–200 px), tight tracking, plus a small mono or caps label system for eyebrows, numbers and metadata.
4. Numbers rows (24 / 12 / 9K+ / 15) with tiny captions; FAQ accordions; stacked/fanned cards; marquee strips.
5. Light used as material: glowing ribbons (Relay green, Laocoon orange/blue), particles (Vesper, Auralis), soft blurred gradients (Creative Director). Always one light source and one colour family per page.
6. Section rhythm: full-bleed dark hero → editorial white or near-black text section → card grid → big statement → footer with large wordmark.
7. Motion: scroll-scrubbed object rotation, text that reveals line by line, counters, cursor-following light, smooth (Lenis-like) scrolling; nothing bounces.
8. Every template shows Performance and SEO panels (Lighthouse 90+/100) and phone/laptop mock-ups: speed and responsiveness are part of the quality.

## Direction chosen for Abidora (original, not copied)
- Keep Abidora's own identity (serif display + mono labels, ink/bone/signal palette, the orbit-dot mark).
- Founder: cut-out portrait of Abid in front of an oversized "ABID ISLAM" wordmark, lit by one warm-magenta rim light that matches the portrait's own neon lighting.
- Tighter nav, one pill CTA, consistent eyebrow/number system, statement sections, stronger footer wordmark.
- Performance first: no heavy new libraries; reduced-motion respected.
