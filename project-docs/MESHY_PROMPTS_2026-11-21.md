# Meshy models for Abidora (Clothing, Sneaker, Bags)

The owner approved Meshy Pro ($20/month) on 21 Nov 2026 (Dhaka time). Cancel after one month if no more models are needed.

Meshy cannot be reached from Claude's build machine (its network blocks meshy.ai). So the owner makes each model in the Meshy website, downloads it as **GLB**, and sends it in the chat. Claude then fits it into the 3D showroom.

## Settings for every model
- Meshy > **Text to 3D** (or **Image to 3D** if you have a photo of your own).
- Turn on **PBR** / realistic textures.
- Style: **Realistic**. Polycount: **Medium** (keeps phones fast).
- After it finishes, press **Download > GLB**.
- Do not use brand names (Nike, Gucci, etc.) in prompts. The designs must be original.

## After download
Send the .glb files in the chat with the model's number (e.g. "4 sneaker"). Claude checks the size and scale, the materials and how it looks on a phone, then adds it to the world with colour and material options.
