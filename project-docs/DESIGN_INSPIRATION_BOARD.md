# Design inspiration board

20 Nov 2026. Which principle from which reference led to which Abidora feature. Basis is how much we could read: the build server could not open any of these sites in a browser, so **none was visually inspected**. "Text" = the site's fetched page text and meta tags; "Public" = its own published case study or credits. Nothing was copied: no code, images, text or layout.

| Abidora feature (built) | Principle we took | From | Basis | What is Abidora's own |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3D Universe home (I-1) | Explore by moving through a world; one persistent object that carries the brand | Bruno Simon, Active Theory | Text, Public | The ora ring as the sun; 12 categories as orbiting systems; worlds as stars that remember your visits; an always-available plain list |
| Emblems (I-4) | Each section recognisable by shape, not only colour | Lusion | Text | Procedural shapes with a rim light, one per category |
| Circle page change (I-2) | Every transition is a designed moment | Igloo Inc, Immersive Garden | Meta, Public | The circle opens from the exact point you clicked |
| World portals (I-3) | Entering a project feels like crossing a threshold | Active Theory, Immersive Garden | Text, Public | 12 small portals, each drawn from its category's idea (compass, blueprint, star ring…) |
| World bar (I-5) | Help, reset and options sit next to the experience | Bruno Simon ("I'm stuck", reset, quality) | Text | Thumb-zone bar; Reset appears only when there is something to reset; Share uses the phone's own share sheet |
| Pinned manifesto, velocity headings (I-6) | One idea per screen; motion that answers the reader | Apple, Lando Norris | Text | Words light up while the statement stays pinned; headings stretch by at most 3 % |
| Founder chapters (I-6) | A personal story told in chapters | Lando Norris | Text | Chapter bar over the owner's five approved sections, unchanged |
| World of the day (I-7) | "Artwork / Image of the day" | Google Arts & Culture, NASA | Text | Same world for everyone on the same date, drawn only from open worlds |
| Guide me (I-7) | A route for people who do not know where to start | Stripe, Figma | Text | Five moods (make, learn, play, real world, wander), unvisited worlds first |
| Surprise me (I-7) | Many doors into the same content | Google Arts & Culture, The Pudding | Text | Never repeats in a visit until every unopened world has been shown |
| Quality tiers (I-8) | Offer quality levels; respect weak devices | Bruno Simon, Active Theory | Text | Automatic tier from the device plus a 1-second probe; Calm and reduced motion always win |
