# Brand guidelines

## Name and lines
- Brand: **Abidora** (editable in Admin → Settings; every page uses the setting).
- Brand line: *A universe of digital experiences.*
- Vision: *A Universe Without Limits.*
- Founder: **Abid Islam**, Founder of Abidora, Digital Creator · Creative Technologist.
- Founder tagline: *"Exploring the possibilities of intelligence, technology and imagination to build what comes next."*

## Mark
The **ora ring**: in the wordmark the last "o" is replaced by a thin ring with an amber dot in orbit (Abid◯ra). The same ring appears as the loader, on the Founder hero and on the 404 page. Do not stretch it, recolour the dot outside the signal colour, or add effects.

## Colour
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ink | #0d0c0b | background |
| ink2 | #161513 | raised surfaces |
| bone | #ece6dc | main text |
| bone2 | #d9d1c4 | secondary text |
| mute | #8d877d | labels |
| signal | #e8b46a | the one accent (changes with Studio mood) |
Each world has its own palette; the shell stays ink/bone/signal.

## Type
- Display and editorial: **Instrument Serif** (italic for emphasis).
- Interface and body: **Inter Tight**.
- Labels, numbers, metadata: **JetBrains Mono**, uppercase, wide tracking.
- Non-Latin scripts use matching Noto serif/sans families.

## Voice
Calm, confident, editorial, international. Short sentences. No hype words, no invented claims, achievements, clients or testimonials. Interests are described as exploration, never as expertise.

## Founder imagery
Use only the owner-approved portrait (cropped, no third-party marks). Never generate or alter his face.
