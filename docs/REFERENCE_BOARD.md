How to read it: **observed** means we read the site's own case study, award page or credits text. **inferred** means we could not open the site from the build server, so the notes come from published descriptions only. We never claim to have visually inspected a site we could not open. References are used for principles only; nothing is copied.

## Details

### Igloo Inc
- Recognition: Awwwards Site of the Year 2024 and Developer Site of the Year 2024; Webby 2026 winner, Best Visual Design (Aesthetic)
- Source: https://awwwards.com/igloo-inc-case-study.html
- Facts: Three-section landing page; real-time shader intro; scene transitions mixing chromatic aberration, displacement and frost; procedural ice models; particle simulation from volume data; WebGL UI with glitch and SDF text scramble; Three.js, Svelte, GSAP; shader compilation spread across load to stay fast.

### Lusion v3
- Recognition: Awwwards Developer Site of the Year 2023
- Source: https://www.awwwards.com/annual-awards-2023/developer-site-of-the-year
- Facts: Jury: clean interface blended with over-the-top animation; 'you don't need crazy fonts or vibrant colors'. Third portfolio version showing experiments and process.

### Lusion (2019 site)
- Recognition: Awwwards Site of the Month, May 2019
- Source: https://awwwards.com/case-study-for-lusion-by-lusion-winner-of-site-of-the-month-may.html
- Facts: Cloth simulation precomputed in Houdini (about 220 KB) and blended by interaction; vertex animation stored in PNG; baked lighting plus matcaps; video synced to real-time render; static site.

### Immersive Garden (studio site, 2025)
- Recognition: Awwwards Studio of the Year 2024
- Source: https://www.awwwards.com/case-study-immersive-gardens-new-website.html
- Facts: Minimal, atmospheric design; bas-relief 3D element; 3D Roman numerals as menu; hidden Backstage section with technical breakdowns; KTX texture compression; Three.js, Nuxt, GSAP, Lenis.

### David Whyte Experience
- Recognition: Listed among the studio's Site of the Day wins (Annual Awards 2024 page)
- Source: https://awwwards.com/case-study-david-whyte-experience-by-immersive-garden.html
- Facts: Real watercolour paintings; cursor drives a watercolour fluid effect; long press reveals a video of the place that inspired the poem; custom shaders in Three.js.

### Orano
- Recognition: Awwwards Site of the Month, November
- Source: https://www.awwwards.com/orano-from-immersive-garden-wins-site-of-the-month-novemeber.html
- Facts: Each product section is a different 3D place; each scroll ends in a small gameplay moment; wireframe line rendering; terrain from image channels; lighter meshes on mobile; RGB and tile offset transitions.

### ATEN7
- Recognition: Awwwards Site of the Month, July 2023
- Source: https://awwwards.com/aten7-by-immersive-garden-wins-sotm-july-2023.html
- Facts: Drag and release moves through four seasons and years; 3D changes in sync with time; custom alphabet made for the site; original sound design.

### Sculpting Harmony
- Recognition: Awwwards Site of the Month, November 2023
- Source: https://www.awwwards.com/sculpting-harmony-wins-site-of-the-month-november-2023.html
- Facts: Scroll advances aerial drone footage along mapped camera paths; Sharp Grotesk type stretched in proportion to scroll; photogrammetry models in Three.js; GSAP ScrollTrigger.

### Active Theory v5
- Recognition: Webby Technical Achievement nominee
- Source: https://www.webbyawards.com/crafted-with-code/active-theory
- Facts: Eight real-time 3D environments, each with a large central screen playing project reels; flickering lights and type for an 'alien' feel; spacebar easter egg switches environments.

### Halo 5 Guardians Visualizer
- Recognition: Case study (no award named on page)
- Source: https://medium.com/@activetheory/xbox-halo-5-guardians-visualizer-5b9afcb542f1
- Facts: Helmet built from WebGL particles; cursor acts as a light source; chromatic aberration tied to rotation speed; depth of field; text shatters into shards between states; quality settings by device tier.

### 20 Years of Xbox Museum
- Recognition: Webby winner (2022 entry)
- Source: https://www.webbyawards.com/crafted-with-code/20-years-of-xbox-museum
- Facts: Six 3D environments with real-time avatars; a personal museum generated from the visitor's own history.
