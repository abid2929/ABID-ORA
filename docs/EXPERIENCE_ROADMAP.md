## Build order
1. Wave 1: one flagship per category (12 worlds), each with its own type, palette, motion and signature interaction. 074 Digital Galaxy is the first.
2. Waves 2–4: the rest, best ideas first.
3. Each world is lazy-loaded: its code and media only download when a visitor opens it.

A world moves to TESTED only after the 10-language × desktop/mobile matrix passes, and to COMPLETED only after the owner signs it off.

## Signature journey (owner, 20 Nov 2026 08:02 UTC)
**ENTER ABIDORA → EXPLORE THE UNIVERSE → DISCOVER WORLDS → TALK TO ABIDORA AI → PERSONALIZE THE EXPERIENCE**, felt as one connected universe, not separate features. Status: PLANNED. It is placed into the existing days without changing their order:
- **Enter** (home, exists). The hero leads straight into "Explore". Later, the living sky from Innovation Backlog idea 6.
- **Explore** (day 15). The universe map (backlog idea 1) becomes the main navigation between the home page and the worlds. The plain list stays for accessibility.
- **Discover** (days 5–15 and 15). Each world ends with "next worlds you may like" and a "Surprise me" option (backlog idea 3), based on themes, not tracking.
- **Talk to Abidora AI** (days 18–22). One consistent, quiet AI button in the same corner on every world and page. It opens a side panel on desktop and a bottom sheet on phones, knows which world you are in, and can open worlds with settings (backlog idea 4). It is keyboard accessible, never covers world controls, and is hidden when the user turns it off. Until real AI keys exist, the panel says plainly that the AI is not connected.
- **Personalize** (Studio exists, days 2–4). Studio choices (theme, cursor, motion, sound, language) apply everywhere. Accounts (days 16–17) sync them along with the passport and favourites.
